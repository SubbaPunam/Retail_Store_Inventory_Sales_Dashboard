1.Project Title
Retail Store Inventory & Sales Dashboard

2.Short Description
An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze retail sales performance, inventory levels, product demand, and pricing trends through KPIs and data visualizations.

3.Tech Stack
Microsoft Power BI
Power Query
DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)

4.Data Source: 
The dataset was downloaded from Kaggle in CSV format and used for analyzing retail sales, inventory levels, product demand, and pricing trends.

5.Features
KPI Cards: Display key inventory and sales metrics.
Monthly Sales Trend: Analyze sales performance over time.
Sales by Category: Compare sales across product categories.
Inventory by Region: Analyze inventory distribution across regions.
Inventory vs. Demand: Compare inventory levels with product demand.
Interactive Slicers: Filter data by region, category, and product.
Data Visualization: Identify sales patterns and inventory trends.
